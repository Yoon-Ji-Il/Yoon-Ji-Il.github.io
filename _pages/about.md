Hi! I am an M.S. student in the Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at **DGIST** (Daegu Gyeongbuk Institute of Science and Technology), which I joined in September 2026. Before that, I received my B.S. in Electronic Engineering from **Inha University**.

My research interests lie at the intersection of human motion and robotics. I am interested in modeling how humans move and in building robots that can perceive the world, understand language, and act on it.

## Research Interests

- Human Motion Modeling
- Robotics
- Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Models

## Education

- **M.S. in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science**, DGIST (2026 – Present)
- **B.S. in Electronic Engineering**, Inha University (2022 – 2026)
